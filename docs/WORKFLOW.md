## P0 Golden Scenario

Customer creates urgent reactive request for M-104.

↓

AI identifies mechanical issue.

↓

AI recommends bearing and required skill.

↓

Backend validates machine/site/request.

↓

SLA is calculated.

↓

Technicians are ranked.

↓

Required parts are checked.

↓

Resources are reserved.

↓

Manager approves.

↓

Technician receives job.

↓

Technician checks in.

↓

Technician executes checklist.

↓

Technician becomes unavailable.

↓

Exception engine opens exception.

↓

Replacement technicians are ranked.

↓

Manager approves reassignment.

↓

Resources are re-reserved.

↓

Replacement technician completes job.

↓

Evidence is submitted.

↓

Completion is verified.

↓

Customer approves.

↓

Request is closed.

↓

Machine passport is updated.

↓

Audit trail records all important actions.
